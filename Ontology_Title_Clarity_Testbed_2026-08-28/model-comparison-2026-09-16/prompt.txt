There are two inputs:
(1) Verb-direct object pairs, derived by extracting those terms from O*NET work activity descriptions. These are called titles and will be used to designate work activities in a knowledge graph. Some are based on a single O*NET description, others on multiple O*NET descriptions.
(2) O*NET description(s) that served as the basis for the related verb-direct object pair.

Produce these outputs:
(A) For verb-direct object pairs based on a single O*NET description, indicate those for which:
(i) Current title conveys a clear, intuitively understandable sense of the work activity depicted in the O*NET description and thus should not be changed.
(ii) Current title does not provide a clear, intuitively understandable sense of the work activity depicted in the O*NET description and thus should be changed; in such cases, create a new title that is semantically transparent, as specific as possible, intuitive to a non-expert, and confined to 2-5 words. Do not change the initial verb in any proposed title.
(B) For verb-direct object pairs based on multiple O*NET descriptions, indicate those for which
(i) O*NET descriptions are similar enough that they warrant being grouped together and current title is adequate per the guidelines provided in (A) and thus should not be changed.
(ii) O*NET descriptions are similar enough that they warrant being grouped together, but the title is inadequate per the guidelines provided in (A) and thus should be changed; in such cases, propose a new title using the guidelines provided in (A).
(iii) O*NET descriptions are different enough that they should be recategorized; in such cases, propose new groupings and create new titles using the guidelines provided in (A). When developing new categories of O*NET descriptions, create groupings of approximately 5-9 O*NET descriptions each, unless there is a strong reason for creating larger or smaller groupings (e.g.  if 10+ O*NET descriptions are identical, they should be kept together). If the current title is suitable for one of the resulting new groupings, indicate as much.
Throughout, focus only on the work activity depicted by the verb-direct object in the title and ignore other verbs and direct objects that appear in the O*NET description. After evaluating each pair of inputs, provide rationale.
