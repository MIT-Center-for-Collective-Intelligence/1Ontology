# Research method and controlled continuation

The frozen ontology supplies activity identity and recorded taxonomy. O*NET task titles identify tasks; they do not prove execution order, completeness, occupational context, safety rules or correctness. Steps bind to existing eligible frozen activity nodes. Collections and bracketed classification questions are never executable activities. Taxonomy specialization is distinct from procedural decomposition; children are not automatically consecutive steps.

Proposed activity titles start with a base-form verb. Collections optionally group specializations under an activity by a dimension; their titles are bracketed questions such as [Act on what?], [Act how?], [Act by whom?], [Act for what purpose?] or [Create information how?]. A collection is a grouping, not an executable sub-activity or an invented intermediate activity. These rules guide future proposals; this capture preserves existing frozen titles and recorded relationships exactly.

Captured procedures preserve the original typed flow, steps, source claims, explicitly unproven hypotheses, objection dispositions and declared change proposals. Sequence, parallel split/join, conditional paths with guards and otherwise branches, loops with progress/termination and recursion with a base case and smaller input are distinct control constructs. Algorithm recursion is distinct from a taxonomy cycle: a taxonomy cycle blocks affected derivations. Multiple ancestry paths and uneven depths must be retained rather than treated as a strict tree.

Historical mechanical/independent research review and source/empirical support are separate dimensions. Two agreeing research reviewers do not establish occupational truth. This handoff verifies public projection conformance and canonical procedure digests only. It does not authenticate the full frozen corpus, native execution receipts, prior-objection receipts or all history. Missing proofs stay unverified; there is no public acceptance gate to bypass them.

## Restart and continuation boundary

Restarting this captured app is safe: it makes no filesystem or ontology writes. The latest handoff policy uses the current Codex app GPT-6.1 Sol session only. Do not launch legacy isolated MIT Codex CLI generation commands, resume a Supervisor, or signal paused workers with SIGCONT from this handoff. Model execution, inference downloads and remote writes are disabled by absence of any such route or runner.

Any future generation continuation needs a separately authorized, source-held environment and one coordinated proposal-store writer. Re-freeze and verify the complete source hash, actual node eligibility, taxonomy SCCs, all parent paths and level queue. Reconcile immutable request keys, uncertain attempts and receipts before retrying; never duplicate an ambiguous active turn. Preserve exact task prompts, public messages, outcomes, bounded retries and independent criticism. The full corpus is required for a new-node absence claim: a search over this projection is insufficient.

Generate and validate every one of the 20,995 terminal activities before any parent generation. Whole-level gates remain mandatory. Each current candidate must pass meaningful mechanical checks and two independent review roles. Preserve atomic/evidence-poor/missing-reference blockers. Do not replace them with generic scaffolds, force acceptance, or count synthetic UI fixtures as research coverage.

Parents derive common flow from child algorithms, with version-bound step/control mappings and specialization exceptions; taxonomy child IDs are not automatically execution steps. On descendant or research graph changes invalidate changed versions and old/new ancestor closures through all parent paths, then rerun affected checks and both reviews. Changes to ontology structure remain separate research proposals, with no production writes or automatic acceptance.

## Known source limitations

Historical first-order display and human/batch-label changes were interrupted and the source app restored its earlier version. The source generator prompt omitted the exact role lexical rule and used generic feedback; this handoff generates no replacements. Historical mutable latest-metadata pointers could couple epochs. The current export omitted some ledger proof while an observer was older; captured status is therefore historical, not a freshly authenticated full execution chain. Four source-suite fixtures required live private context and remained held. This package does not claim those historical defects were repaired, replayed or waived.

The public capture excludes private native/session/account data, raw SQL stores, full corpus, caches and raw logs. Public canonical procedures remain unchanged, but privacy transformations alter the enclosing public capture digest. Consult the transformation manifest rather than equating those digests.
