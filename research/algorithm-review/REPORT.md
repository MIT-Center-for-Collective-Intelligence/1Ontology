## Review of the handoff, 13 October

The package is sound as a read-only record, but the eight algorithms are weaker than their passing reviews suggest. The full findings are in `data/review-2026-10-13.json` and in the app.

- All eight flows are plain sequences. No real algorithm uses parallel steps, conditions, loops or recursion, so that part of the method is untested on real data. Four would be better described otherwise, and the app shows a proposed revision for each: the two map and screen readings and the two environmental assessments are independent of each other (parallel), supervising research repeats (loop), and checking a design only matters if a failed check leads back to redesign (loop).
- The only evidence is the O*NET task title. The occupation the task belongs to is not recorded, although it changes what the procedure looks like.
- All 16 captured critiques pass with no objections. They check that identities match and that hypotheses are labeled, not whether the flow is right.
- Four leaves use their own taxonomy parents as steps; for example "Order, unload, and store feed and supplies" sits under Order Supply, Unload Supply and Store Supply and uses all three as its steps. Deriving those parents upward from their children would then be circular. How such tasks are placed must be decided before parent generation.
- "Collect (information) (Information)" mixes collecting money (bets, fares, rent, taxes), collecting information, collecting items and compiling. A [Collect what?] grouping is proposed.

The documented continuation cannot be run from this repository: it needs the private pipeline store, the frozen corpus and its importer, none of which are included. Generation therefore remains where the capture left it.
